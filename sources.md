## Public data for building the shade map (checked 2026-09-26)
- ✅ [IMPLAN Chihuahua downloads](https://implanchihuahua.org/Descargables_DA.html). Includes `Construcciones_2007` (**655,120 buildings with number of floors**, UTM 13N), `Banquetas_2016` (sidewalks), `AreaVerde_2016`, `ParquesyJardineras_2007`, `Manzanas_2016`, `RedVial_2016`.
  - [Construcciones_2007.zip (68 MB)](https://implanchihuahua.org/Descargables/da/shp/080190001_Construcciones_2007.zip). `PISOS` field: 1, 2, 3, 4, L, EC, T (L/EC/T: meaning still to verify).
- ✅ [IMPLAN route maps (PDF, 2023–2024)](https://implanchihuahua.org/IMPLAN-Datos/Rutas/). BRT, CHIHUAHUA-PORTILLO, NORTE, SUR, PONIENTE, CIRCUNVALATORIA. Only lines, no stops.
- [INEGI Censo 2020, urban environment](https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/ccpv/2020/). Per block face: "Parada de transporte colectivo" and "Árboles o palmeras". [Questionnaire](https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/programas/ccpv/2020/doc/Censo2020_cuest_entorno.pdf) · [Methodology](https://www.inegi.org.mx/contenidos/productos/prod_serv/contenidos/espanol/bvinegi/productos/nueva_estruc/702825199425.pdf)
- [Meta/WRI canopy height 1 m](https://registry.opendata.aws/dataforgood-fb-forests/). Tree height (imagery from 2018–2020).
- [Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/GOOGLE_Research_open-buildings-temporal_v1). Building heights up to 2023.
- [Mapillary](https://www.mapillary.com/). Free street-level photos (coverage in Chihuahua not verified).
- OpenStreetMap downtown: 685 buildings (3 with a height), 29 stops. Very incomplete.

## Business opportunity engine (OVIE and others), researched 2026-09-26
- [OVIE: ovie.mpiochih.gob.mx](https://ovie.mpiochih.gob.mx/). Office for economic information (Municipality + IMPLAN + INEGI). Indicators by area inferred from its code: population, households, age, schooling, Social Development Index 2020 (block/neighborhood), economic units by sector and business type, and **per business type within the area: average monthly sales, salaries, water and electricity costs** (Economic Census 2024, INEGI microdata). PDF/Excel export. No public API documented.
- [OVIE reaches 1,300 users – El Heraldo](https://oem.com.mx/elheraldodechihuahua/local/plataforma-ovie-alcanza-mil-300-usuarios-herramienta-clave-para-emprendedores-y-urbanistas-22428800) · [Agreement signed with INEGI – Quadratín](https://chihuahua.quadratin.com.mx/principal/firman-convenio-para-impulsar-plataforma-ovie/) · [Invita Municipio a conocer OVIE](https://www.municipiochihuahua.gob.mx/CCS/Prensa/Invita_Gobierno_Municipal_a_conocer_la_plataforma_OVIE)
